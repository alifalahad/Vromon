**App name** (30): `Vromon`

**Short description** (80):
`Plan trips, map every country you've visited, and compare maps with friends.`

**Full description** (4000):

Vromon plans your trips and keeps the map of everywhere you have been.

**Your travel map**
- Mark the countries, regions and cities you've visited, want to visit, or lived in.
- See how much of the world you've explored, by country, region and city.
- Choose a map theme: complete themes, colour palettes and map styles.

**Plan trips**
- Plan a trip in six steps: where, when, budget, interests, pace and schedule.
- Get a day-by-day itinerary built around opening hours, travel time and your budget, then adapt it.
- Add your route (plane, bus, train, car, boat or walking) and see every trip's duration.
- Trip statistics: total travel days, average trip length, countries and continents.
- When a trip ends, mark its places on your map in one tap.

**Travel with friends**
- Add friends by username, QR code or invite link.
- See your friends' maps, photos and trips, and compare maps to find the places you have in common.
- Your account is private: only friends you accept see your map.

**Photos**
- Add photos to the places and trips they belong to. Location data is removed from every photo.

Vromon is free, has no ads, and lets you export or delete your data at any time.
