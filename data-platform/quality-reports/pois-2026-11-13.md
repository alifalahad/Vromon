## Categories

- Every Overture category in this release is mapped (`source-registry/poi-kinds.yaml`). The only unmapped rows are
  the 14,517 in the box with **no category at all**: their kind comes from words in the name (college, bazar,
  মসজিদ…), else "other".
- Generic categories made specific by the name: "Govt. Brajalal College" (place_of_learning → college), "বড় বাজার"
  (shopping → market), "Sonadanga Bus Stand" (transit → bus station), "Rupsha Launch Ghat" (travel → ferry terminal).
